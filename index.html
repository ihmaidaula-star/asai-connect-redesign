04 / A SPACE BUILT ON TRUST

Nothing you bring here should be taken lightly.

Privacy and safety are essential to meaningful care. Understand the commitments Asai makes to its community.

01

Your story deserves privacy.

Your personal experiences deserve to be treated with care, dignity and appropriate confidentiality.

02

Your choices matter.

You should understand how your information is handled and have meaningful control over your participation.

03

Care requires accountability.

Professional support should be guided by appropriate standards, informed consent and safeguarding practices.

Confidentiality has legal and safeguarding limits, particularly where there is a serious risk of harm.
