01 / OUR STORY
✳

Wellness, reimagined for Africa.

For too many people, finding quality wellness support feels overwhelming. Services can be disconnected, expensive or designed without fully considering the cultures, communities and realities of the people they serve.

Asai believes there is a better way. You are a whole person. Your care should be too. Your experiences, your identity, your relationships, your culture and your aspirations all deserve to be part of the conversation.

04 / A SPACE BUILT ON TRUST

Nothing you bring here should be taken lightly.

Privacy is not just a policy page. It is an important part of creating an environment where people can feel safe enough to seek support.

01

If you're seeking care

Your story deserves privacy.

The original Asai platform states that sessions, journal entries and community shares are not sold or used for advertising.

02

Your visibility, your choice.

Participate on your terms.

The original platform describes options such as using a chosen name in communities, keeping your camera off and managing your personal account.

03

If you're a specialist

Professional accountability.

Asai describes credential checks, protected personal contact details, consent records and clinical governance pathways.

Confidentiality is not absolute. Serious risk of harm and legal obligations may require information to be shared. Review the platform's current privacy policy before relying on any specific privacy or security commitment.
